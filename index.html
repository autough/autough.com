What we've built

Tools we've built for office jobs we keep seeing in Atlantic Canadian businesses. Each one runs on the files the business already has.

Grocery-chain deductions

For food plants paid by remittance. It reads the remittance file (PDF, CSV or EDI), sorts every deduction into disputable, check-the-backup or valid, catches deductions taken twice, and drafts the dispute.

Seafood export paperwork

For exporters filing catch certificates. A working prototype that chases boats and buying stations for licence and landing details and builds the file for each load.

Overtime checks for multi-location teams

It reads scheduling and payroll exports and checks hours against each province's overtime rules, location by location, before payroll goes out.

Daily reports from point-of-sale email

For restaurants: the reports your point-of-sale system already emails become one morning summary, so nobody has to pull the numbers together by hand.
